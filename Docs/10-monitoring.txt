# Monitoring, Audit & Dashboards (Req 29)

Version: 1.0 · Owner: SRE + Data Platform

## 1. Objective

Provide centralized, real-time visibility into:
- File ingestion health (per channel, per region)
- Duplicate prevention effectiveness
- Transfer success and latency to Databricks
- Reconciliation status between PostgreSQL and Databricks
- Watcher health and checkpoint progression
- Authentication and access anomalies

All metrics persist to `fvi.monitor_metrics` and are surfaced via
`fvi.dashboard_config` panels rendered in Grafana or Superset.
